Participation & Supply Fee

Co-op meets every Tuesday, and every family teaches, co-teaches, or volunteers. There is no tuition — one supply fee per family, per year, waived for families who lead a class.

Leading a class — fee waived
Co-teaching, volunteering, or admin support — $200 due
Check — payable to Damian Brockway, memo line “for supplies”
Cash — to a co-op leader, who gives you a receipt

Every dollar goes back to the host church or to classroom supplies and equipment. Nothing is kept as profit and no one is paid from it. This is a participation fee, not a tax-deductible donation — please don’t claim it as one.

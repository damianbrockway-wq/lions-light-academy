# Lions Light Academy
## Supply List
### What to bring on the first day

**2026–2027 School Year | Version 1.0**

> Short on purpose. If it isn't on this list, you don't need to buy it.

---

## Every student, every age

- **A Bible** — any translation
- **One notebook**
- **Pens and pencils**
- **A folder or thin binder** for papers coming home
- **A bag** to keep it all in
- **A water bottle**
- **A snack**
- **Lunch** — from the second Tuesday on. The first day is on us.

**Put a name on everything.**

---

## Middle School and High School — one more thing

**Access to a computer, laptop, Chromebook or tablet at home.**

Not for co-op day — for the work in between. Assignments are posted in Google Classroom, and some are typed or turned in online.

A shared family computer is fine. It doesn't need to belong to the student.

If a device at home is a problem, tell us. We'd rather solve it quietly than have a student fall behind over hardware.

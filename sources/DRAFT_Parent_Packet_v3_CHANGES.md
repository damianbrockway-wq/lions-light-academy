# 3. Discipline Approach — replace in full

This is the section that needs real work. It currently reads:

> **Discipline Approach**
> We use restorative practices that focus on reconciliation, reflection, and personal growth rather than punitive measures.

That's true but it's now the only thing a family reads in the packet about behavior, and it promises restoration without mentioning that participation can end. A parent who reads only this and then meets the Code of Conduct at signing has been surprised, and that's the surprise worth avoiding.

**Replace with:**

> **Discipline Approach**
>
> Our approach is restorative. We correct in order to keep a student, not to build a case against them, and the goal after any correction is always to bring the student back in.
>
> Restoration is real and it is offered to every student. It is also not automatic. A student who has done damage owns what happened, makes repair, and then meets the terms of a written plan before they are restored. An apology by itself does not finish it.
>
> Behavior is handled at three levels — minor, moderate, and severe. The first and second moderate concerns in a school year each carry a documented warning in writing to you. Warnings count across all classes, not per teacher, and reset each school year. A third moderate concern in a school year, or any severe incident, begins the Reconciliation Process. The full process is set out in the Escalation & Discipline Policy.
>
> **Learning is the student's work, and nobody can do it for them.** Teachers teach and parents support, but the student shows up ready, attempts the work, and stays engaged. Lions Light Academy is a co-op of active learners, and a student who will not take part in learning cannot remain in the program. We say that plainly here so that it is never a surprise later.
>
> Our teachers know their students and will use judgment when something real is going on in a child's life. That judgment belongs to the teacher. Hard weeks happen and are met with grace, but a hard week is not a standing exemption from the work.
>
> The Code of Conduct and the Escalation & Discipline Policy govern this in full. Both are in the Resources library on our website, and both are signed at enrollment.

# 5. Attendance and Participation — one added paragraph

This section currently covers parent participation only (teaching or volunteering one day a week). Student participation now has expectations too, and this is where a parent would look for them.

**Add beneath the existing paragraph:**

> Students are expected to participate as well. A student who comes to class without work the lesson depends on, or falls behind during it, may be asked to finish it in the hallway or lounge and rejoin the class when it is done. They remain supervised throughout. This is not a punishment and nothing is recorded. Work not completed for two class sessions in a row is handled as a moderate concern under the Escalation & Discipline Policy.

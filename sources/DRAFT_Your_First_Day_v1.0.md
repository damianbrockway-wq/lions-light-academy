# Lions Light Academy
## Your First Day
### What the first Tuesday actually looks like

**2026–2027 School Year | Version 1.0**

> The first day is not a normal Tuesday. Here's what to expect, so nothing is a surprise.

---

## Arrive at 8:45

Belongings go neatly along the entryway wall, then students head straight to the Main Room.

Teachers are in the building from 8:30. There's no need to arrive earlier than 8:45 — we'll still be setting up.

---

## The first hour is different

**We open with worship and prayer over the year.** Everyone together, and every adult stands and is named, so your student knows who's who by the end of it.

**Then we walk the fire route.** On foot, the whole way, to the shed behind the building. Attendance is taken there. Every student should be able to answer *"where do we go?"* without being told — that's the point of doing it on day one rather than reading it off a page.

**Then expectations** — the same talk for every age. Students in **grade 7 and up sign their own pledge in the room**. **Grade 6 and under bring it home** for a parent to sign and return the following week.

---

## Classes are short on the first day

That opening hour replaces first period. The first class starts at **10:00**, and the blocks run shorter than usual so everything still fits.

Nobody falls behind because of it. The normal schedule begins the second Tuesday.

---

## Lunch is on us

**The first day only.** From the second Tuesday onward, students bring their own lunch.

Snacks come from home every week, including the first.

---

## Pickup at 1:45

Cleanup starts at **1:30 sharp**, and every student has a station — it's part of the day, not an afterthought. We close with prayer, then dismiss at 1:45.

---

- **Google Classroom** — get the app and join the class before Tuesday if you can

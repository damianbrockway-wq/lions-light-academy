# Lions Light Academy
## Code of Conduct
### Faith-Based Expectations for Students and Families

**Effective: September 15, 2026 | Version 1.2**

> **This version supersedes Version 1.1.** Every enrolled family signs this version.

---

## Purpose & Authority

Lions Light Academy (LLA) exists to provide a Christ-centered, orderly learning environment where students can grow academically, socially, and spiritually.

This Code applies to all co-op days, activities on church property, all off-site events, and LLA class group chats and shared online spaces.

---

## Core Values

- Safety is a primary priority, upheld through supervision and clear expectations
- Respect for adults, peers, and property
- Faith-based behavior reflecting Christian character
- Order and accountability that support learning
- Grace in correction once safety and order are established

---

## Student Expectations

Students are expected to follow instructions from teachers and supervising adults, use appropriate language, respect boundaries, comply with technology rules, and remain in supervised areas unless given permission otherwise.

---

## The Student's Responsibility

**Learning is the student's work, and nobody can do it for them.**

Teachers teach. Parents support. The student shows up ready, attempts the work, and stays engaged in the room. That responsibility belongs to the student and it does not transfer to anyone else.

LLA is a co-op of active learners. A student who will not carry their part of it isn't being served here, and is taking instruction away from students who are.

**About personal circumstances.** Our teachers know their students and will use judgment when something real is going on. That judgment belongs to the teacher. It is not something a student invokes to get out of work. Hard weeks happen and are met with grace. A hard week is not a standing exemption, and a student who reaches for one every week is showing us the problem rather than the reason.

---

## Unfinished Work

A student who comes to class without the work the lesson depends on, or falls behind during it, may be sent to finish it in the hallway or lounge and rejoin the class when it's done.

They stay supervised. The adult in the main room supervises the hallway, and a student uses the teachers lounge only when an adult is already in it. If the lounge is empty, no student is in it either.

**This is not a punishment or a warning.** Nothing is recorded and it counts toward nothing. It protects the lesson for the students who are ready for it.

**Work not done for two class sessions in a row is a moderate concern,** and the teacher raises it.

---

## How Correction Works

The full Escalation & Discipline Policy governs behavior at LLA and is in the Resources library. In summary:

**Minor** — disruptions, not following instructions, inappropriate language or tone, misuse of technology. Corrected in the moment, not counted. A teacher may raise a persistent pattern to moderate.

**Moderate** — repeated minor issues, disrespect, disruption that interferes with instruction, not complying after correction, work not done two sessions running, or refusing to take part. The first and second in a school year each carry a documented warning to you in writing. A **third** begins the Reconciliation Process. Warnings count across all classes and reset each year.

**Severe** — anything unsafe, aggressive, or threatening, and defiance that puts the room at risk. **No warning.** The student is removed from the activity immediately and stays supervised, you are contacted that day, and the Reconciliation Process begins.

Severity determines the response. A serious incident never waits behind a warning count, and small things never become severe by adding up.

---

## The Reconciliation Process

**Our goal is never to remove a student.** It is to keep every student here and learning.

1. **Pause** — the student steps out of the activity, the day, or the program
2. **Meeting** — student, parent, teacher, and leadership, in person or by video. If your student disputes what happened, leadership hears everyone here and decides what it finds before anything else
3. **Ownership** — the student names what happened and who it affected, in their own words
4. **Repair** — a specific action toward those affected
5. **Re-Entry Plan** — specific expectations in writing, signed by student and parent
6. **Review Period** — four co-op days your student is present; absences extend it
7. **Restoration** — confirmed in writing, or extended once, or participation ends

**What restoration requires.** The signed plan is the standard. Take ownership, make repair, meet the plan, and your student is restored. You'll know exactly what's being asked because you sign it.

**Completing it clears the count.** A restored student returns with zero warnings.

**If a family won't take part,** the process can't be completed and participation ends. We'll have made reasonable attempts to schedule and given you written notice first.

---

## Students Unwilling to Learn

**A student who will not take part in learning cannot remain at LLA.**

We say it plainly so no family is surprised later. A student may be asked to leave where they repeatedly do not attempt assigned work, decline to take part in instruction, or continue not participating after the Reconciliation Process has been completed.

The Leadership Team decides this, on the record of what actually happened, never on one adult's opinion of a single bad day. Except where immediate safety requires otherwise, your student is offered the Reconciliation Process before this decision is made.

A student who has decided not to learn doesn't only lose their own year. They take instruction from the students beside them and from the volunteer parent teaching the class. We would rather lose one enrollment than let a classroom stop working for everyone in it.

---

## Teacher Authority

Teachers and supervising adults may correct behavior immediately and temporarily remove a student from class or an activity to maintain order and safety.

Teachers decide when a pattern has become a moderate concern and when work has not been done. Leadership, not teachers, decides restoration and removal.

---

## Parent Partnership

LLA operates as a cooperative community. Parents partner with teachers and leadership in addressing behavioral concerns and supporting corrective actions. Where the Reconciliation Process applies, the meeting is required and re-entry plans are signed by both student and parent.

**If you disagree** with a warning or a decision, ask leadership to review it. You'll get an answer in writing.

---

## One Exception

Where immediate safety requires it, a student may be removed without the Reconciliation Process first.
